BCS 113: ELECTRONICS

Detailed Study Notes

Electronics is the study of how electric current is controlled and used in circuits to perform useful tasks. It is the foundation of modern computing, communication, automation, and electrical devices.

1. Introduction to Electronics

Electronics focuses on the flow and control of electric charges in components, circuits, and semiconductor devices. It is essential in power systems, communication systems, computers, and household technology.

3. Ohm's Law

Ohm's law states that current through a conductor is directly proportional to voltage and inversely proportional to resistance.

V = IR

Example: If R = 10Ω and I = 2A, then V = 2 × 10 = 20V.

4. Electrical Components

4.1 Resistors

Resistors limit current and help set voltage levels in a circuit.

4.2 Capacitors

Capacitors store charge and are used in smoothing, timing, and filtering circuits.

4.3 Inductors

Inductors resist changes in current and store energy in a magnetic field.

4.4 Diodes

A diode allows current to flow in one direction only and is widely used in rectification.

Key point: A diode acts like a one-way valve for electric current.

4.5 Transistors

Transistors are semiconductor devices used for switching and amplification.

6. Series and Parallel Circuits

6.1 Series Circuit

In a series circuit, current follows a single path. The same current flows through each component.

R_total = R1 + R2 + R3

6.2 Parallel Circuit

In a parallel circuit, components are connected across the same voltage source and the current splits among branches.

1/R_total = 1/R1 + 1/R2 + 1/R3

7. AC and DC

TypeMeaningExample

8. Power and Energy

Power is the rate at which electrical energy is used or transferred.

P = VI

Other forms include:

P = I^2R and P = V^2/R

Example: If a device works at 12V and 2A, then P = 12 × 2 = 24W.

9. Semiconductors and Diodes

Semiconductor diodes are made from p-n junction material.

  • Forward bias: diode conducts current
  • Reverse bias: diode blocks current

10. Applications of Electronics

  • Power supplies
  • Mobile phones and computers
  • Telecommunication systems
  • Medical devices
  • Industrial automation

11. Summary

Electronics involves the controlled movement of electrical charge through circuits and semiconductor devices. The basic principles of voltage, current, resistance, power, and circuit design are essential for understanding how modern technological systems work.
